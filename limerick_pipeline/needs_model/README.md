# HDS 2040 needs model: land tabs

`Limerick-HDS-2040-needs-model.xlsx` is the Spatialis needs model with three tabs added:
- **Zoned land vs need** (`hds_land_tab.py`): homes needed 2026–2040 by catchment against the land register's identified supply, valued at Council densities, with the additional land needed to 2040 and with headroom. The remainder of the county sits below the total and is not analysed.
- **Opportunity sites & options** (`hds_options_tab.py`): what the opportunity sites register could add, what is left for infill on sites not yet identified, and the land that infill needs at Council density or at a higher city infill density (a lever). Catchments with constrained infill, currently Kings Island for flood risk, are flagged so their remainder can be placed elsewhere.
- **Pipeline & permissions needed** (`hds_pipeline_tab.py`, working in `Pipeline calc`): the register's permissions by status and expected completions for a chosen place, and the homes that need permission each year to meet the target. It uses the same month-by-month method and defaults as the tracker's forecasting tool, with yellow levers for the status date, whether to take off expected completions, decision time, time to start, build time, the share never started and the refusal rate. Results by year, a split by bedrooms and tenure from the place's own tab, and all places compared. `pipe_check.py` reruns the tracker's calculation in Python and compares it with the workbook: they match exactly on the defaults.

To rebuild: `python hds_land_tab.py <model.xlsx> tmp.xlsx && python hds_options_tab.py tmp.xlsx tmp2.xlsx && python hds_pipeline_tab.py tmp2.xlsx <out.xlsx>`. Then recalculate in Excel or LibreOffice. The supply and opportunity figures are snapshots of the land register and the opportunity sites register; the need figures are live formulas.
